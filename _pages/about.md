Bio
-----
I was born in Texas, but mostly raised in New Jersey. I completed my undergraduate studies in Cell Biology and Neuroscience at Rutgers University. Afterwards, I worked in a proteomics group at Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, where I developed a strong interest in computational biology. So I returned to school, completing a Masters in Biomedical Informatics at Columbia University, and then a PhD thesis at Mount Sinai. I worked in Eric Sobie's lab, where I optimized a computational pipeline to generate virtual twins for iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes. Now, I am back in NJ as a postdoctoral fellow in mathematical and computational biology, working to link mechanistic and phenomenological models of neuron behavior. In my free time, I enjoy trying new foods and restaurants, working out (except leg day), and relaxing with my friends and my cat.
